Procedural questions

Objections, answered

Is the verdict legally binding?

Legally? No. Socially? Absolutely. A ruling from this court has ended arguments that therapy could not. Carry your engraved verdict card with pride.

Do I need a photo of the defendant?

No — it helps the broadcast department, but name and charges suffice. The defendant may also submit their own defense photo after being served, which roughly doubles the drama.

How fast do I get my verdict?

Judge verdicts are free and ruled within 24 hours (median: 41 minutes). Jury trials take up to 24 hours while the public votes — your jury is the entire internet, empaneled by summons link. Democracy, but pettier.

Can the loser appeal?

Yes — appeals are $3 and succeed approximately never. Counter-suits are also $3 and actively encouraged. This is, frankly, the business model.

Can I file a case as a gift?

Absolutely. File against a friend, gift them the verdict card, then pre-pay $3 for their inevitable appeal. The court salutes your pettiness.

What if the defendant can't afford to defend themselves?

The court anticipated poverty as a legal strategy. At checkout, choose Pay it forward: +$3 covers the accused's response or appeal. Justice waits for no wallet — and now, neither do they.
